# NInfer uplift reports and relevance to Sage — 2026-10-30

The reported 40–100% gains are real project-reported measurements under particular conditions. None below establishes the gain for the local Sage IQ4_XS model on the modified RTX 2080 Ti. They should motivate targeted testing, not be added together as independent gains. All links are pinned primary project documentation; the project authors' numbers have not been reproduced here.

## Local evidence and next investigations

The same-model October 30 study measured llama.cpp A at 95.32/94.53, native Strata B at 107.95/108.50, and Strata with an adapted NInfer-all IQ4_XS expert kernel D at 115.10/115.90 tok/s for arithmetic/Python. D versus B is +6.62%/+6.82%, with no speculation, FP16 KV, 32K configured capacity and graph option enabled. Four warmed samples per variant, two starts, and a small prompt set limit generalization. Device counters and a 16-position full-logit comparison support the tested kernel's correctness and coverage; they do not qualify every possible workload. These are not standalone NInfer results. The short requests do not occupy 32K context.

1. **Standalone C:** map the exact Sage tensor names, dimensions, encoding and model configuration to a byte-preserving Qwen MoE artifact, validate every tensor and metadata, build the SM75 runtime, and compare logits, generation and quality before running a balanced same-model A/B/C/D benchmark. Existing format support is not enough by itself.
2. **GPU timeline first:** measure decode and prefill kernel durations and launch gaps, expert gate/up/down, GDN/attention, output head, and graph replay with representative short and occupied-context workloads. The ranking of these costs on this system is not yet established.
3. **Kernel opportunities conditional on the profile:** investigate fused expert gate/up plus SiLU to reduce launch and intermediate traffic, improve IQ4_XS data movement and scheduling for SM75, and evaluate wider-batch/prefill GGUF MMQ or other compatible tensor-core paths. Validate exact represented quantization, layouts, and numerical behavior. The current adapted expert kernel is a subset of the donor engine, not its complete execution strategy.
4. **Whole-engine paths:** assess GDN/attention, output-head, memory scheduling and graph-compatible batching as measured hotspots, rather than attributing all differences to the expert decoder. Model and GPU-specific route profiles need their own measurements.
5. **MTP as a separately labeled experiment:** verify draft weights and implementation, output equivalence, acceptance rate, total memory and expert residency, then compare identical prompts with speculation off/on. Sweep draft depth and context; record first-token time and quality as well as decode. The examples above show that acceptance and cache pressure can change the outcome dramatically.
6. **Keep effects distinct:** A-to-D compares whole engines with different internals, B-to-D isolates the candidate flag, and MTP changes the decoding mode. Neither changing graph mode nor combining independent benchmark percentages demonstrates a 40% or 100% same-configuration uplift.
